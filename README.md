 Power-BI-Dashboard

 📊 Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard

 Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze business performance across countries, sales channels, product categories, and time periods. It transforms sales data into meaningful insights through KPI cards and interactive visualizations.

 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor key sales performance indicators.
- Analyze revenue and profit trends across months.
- Identify top- and bottom-performing countries by revenue.
- Compare revenue contributions across different sales channels.
- Understand revenue distribution across product categories.
- Enable dynamic analysis using interactive slicers.

 📌 Key Features
- KPI Cards: Total Orders, Total Order Quantity, Total Revenue, Total Gross Profit, and Gross Margin.
- Monthly Analysis: Visual comparison of monthly revenue and sales activity.
- Country Performance: Top 5 and bottom 5 countries based on revenue.
- Sales Channel Analysis: Revenue comparison across Online Store, Corporate, Retail Partners, and Marketplace.
- Product Category Analysis: Revenue distribution across Hiking, Camping, Travel, Cycling, and Fitness.
- Interactive Filters: Filter dashboard data by Year, Quarter, Month, and Country.
- Geographical Visualization: Map-based representation of country-level sales performance.

4. Use the slicers and visualizations to explore sales performance.
